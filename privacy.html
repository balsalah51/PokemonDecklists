Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Pokémon Decklists ("we," "us," or "this site") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit pokemondecklists.com, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

Automatically collected information: Like most websites, we automatically collect certain information when you visit, including your IP address, browser type, device type, pages viewed, and time spent on the site. This is collected through cookies, log files, and similar technologies.

Information you provide: If you join a Discord we later announce, or contact us directly, any information you share there is subject to that platform's own privacy policy, not this one.

We do not require account creation or collect personal information such as your name, email address, or payment details through this site.

Cookies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to understand how visitors use the site (analytics), remember basic preferences, and support advertising.

You can disable cookies through your browser settings. Doing so may affect some site functionality.

Advertising

This site displays advertisements through Google AdSense (publisher ca-pub-1074015774205047). Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google's Ads Settings and review How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Affiliate partnerships

Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you buy through them, we may earn a commission. That does not change the price you pay.

Amazon. We are an Amazon Associate. The Shop links to Amazon for sleeves, dice, playmats, deck boxes, and table extras, and we earn from qualifying purchases.

TCGplayer. We are a TCGplayer affiliate (Impact partner 7670706 / 1780961). Buy links on decklists, the price tracker, and collectibles card pages go to TCGplayer, and we may earn a commission if you purchase after clicking them.

Other retailer programs listed on Partners are application links only until an account is approved. We do not invent tracking IDs for programs we have not joined.

Price tools stored on your device

Watchlist, binder quantities, paid prices, and alert thresholds are saved in your browser with localStorage under pkdl-market-v1. They are not sent to our servers. Clearing site data removes them.

Analytics

We may use third-party analytics services (such as Google Analytics) to understand site traffic. This data is used in aggregate and is not used to personally identify you.

Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
