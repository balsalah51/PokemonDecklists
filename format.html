Format rules

Lists on this site are tagged by format. Standard is the championship pile. Pocket, GLC, Expanded, and vintage Unlimited sit next to it instead of under a leader name.

Standard 2026

H / I / J

G-mark cards left Standard on 26 March 2026 for Pokémon TCG Live and 10 April 2026 for paper Play! Pokémon events. Mega Evolution Pokémon ex follow normal evolution rules and award three Prize cards.

Official: Play! Pokémon rules.
