Window report · 2026-09-11

The Desk

A fan journal sitting on 1,172 public lists. Not a rumor mill. Not a shopfront wearing a magazine costume. The tables first, then the sentences.

Worlds 2026 is over. The paper is still Standard: H, I, and J. Hedrick’s Dragapult is the headline and, in this window, also the plurality — one hundred Standard lists under that name, plus Dusknoir and Blaziken cousins. Alakazam / Dudunsparce, Basic Box, N’s Zoroark, and Slowking are the next seats, not a surprise “dead format.”

Pocket is a different sport. 320 lists, Mega Lucario and Mega Altaria at the front. If you drive to a League Challenge with a 20-card screenshot, you will be illegal. Read Pocket vs paper before you sleeve either.

GLC remains the gym: singleton, one type. Psychic and Colorless posted the most. Expanded is a small table (24 lists). Unlimited is the vintage cups (71). They belong here because they posted, not because they are Standard.

This window · 1 Aug – 9 Sep 2026

The room after Worlds

A fan table of 1,172 public lists. Standard is 678 of them. Pocket is 320 — a Mega Evolution cup, not paper with fewer cards. GLC 79, Unlimited 71, Expanded 24. Worlds 2026 accounts for 254 rows. Andrew Hedrick won Masters on Dragapult; the weeks of online cups after San Francisco are here too.

Biggest 7-day swing in the tracker: Metang +145.8% at $10.47. Most-copied Pokémon line in this window: Dreepy (795 copies).
