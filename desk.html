Window report · 2026-10-07

The Desk

A fan journal sitting on 6,172 public lists. Not a rumor mill. Not a shopfront wearing a magazine costume. The tables first, then the sentences.

Worlds 2026 is over. The paper is still Standard: H, I, and J. Hedrick’s Dragapult is the headline. In this window, 471 Standard lists are filed under that name alone, plus Dusknoir and Blaziken cousins. Next seats: Dragapult Dusknoir, Alakazam Dudunsparce, N's Zoroark, Mega Excadrill, Basic Box. That is a table, not a surprise “dead format.”

Pocket is a different sport. 1898 lists, Mega Lucario and Mega Altaria at the front. If you drive to a League Challenge with a 20-card screenshot, you will be illegal. Read Pocket vs paper before you sleeve either.

GLC remains the gym: singleton, one type. Psychic and Colorless posted the most. Expanded is a small table (24 lists). Unlimited is the vintage cups (154). They belong here because they posted, not because they are Standard.

This window · 1 Aug 2026 – 7 Oct 2026

The room after Worlds

A fan table of 6,172 public lists from August–October 2026. Standard is 3928 of them. Pocket is 1898 - a Mega Evolution cup, not paper with fewer cards. GLC 168, Unlimited 154, Expanded 24. Worlds 2026 accounts for 266 rows. Andrew Hedrick won Masters on Dragapult; the cups from September 2026 onward are here too.

Biggest 7-day swing in the tracker: Metang +145.8% at $10.47. Most-copied Pokémon line in this window: Dreepy (3770 copies).
