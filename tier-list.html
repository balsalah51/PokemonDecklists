Tier list FAQ

How is this Pokémon TCG tier list built?

Worlds 2026 top 24 counts three times. September Play cups count once. That keeps the San Francisco table in front without ignoring the weeks after.

What format is the tier list?

2026 Standard: regulation marks H, I, and J. Mega Evolution–era cards are in the pool.
