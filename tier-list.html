Tier list FAQ

How is this Pokémon TCG tier list built?

Worlds 2026 rows count three times. Standard cups from September 2026 onward count once. San Francisco stays weighted, and a deck that kept winning after Worlds can still climb.

What format is the tier list?

2026 Standard: regulation marks H, I, and J. Mega Evolution–era cards are in the pool.
